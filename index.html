Meet Coach Vimal.

I am Coach Vimal, a wellness and fitness coach with 16 years of experience helping people improve their health, fitness, nutrition and lifestyle habits.

Through VK Fitness & Wellness Center, I combine personalised wellness guidance, nutrition education, physical fitness and sustainable daily habits to help people live healthier.

My approach is simple: understand your body, set realistic goals, and work at them consistently, together, until better health and well-being feel normal.

Vision, mission and values

Our vision
